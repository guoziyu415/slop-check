    The news behind it

    A 2.5 GB model that unlearned the habits

    On October 19 Erik Markus Kannike shared qwen3-4b-deslop. A small LoRA on Qwen3-4B, trained for 1 to 3 hours on an M2 MacBook Air, plus a sign flip on four neurons, took most of the chatbot habits out of its writing. He scored 40 held out answers with his own list of habit patterns.
